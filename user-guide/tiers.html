Tiers

Per-kit skill tiers (LT5 to HT1) earned through tier tests, with readiness bars, a tier leaderboard and dialog menus. Every part can be switched on or off in tiers.yml.

Overview

Tiers run next to the existing ELO ranks. Each tracked kit has its own hidden rating, five readiness bars and a tier. When every required bar is full (by default: enough matches against enough different players), the player can take a tier test: a first-to-3 match against another player testing for the same tier, or against a staff tester. A win gives one tier above the tier your rating shows; a loss gives the tier your rating shows, never lower than your current tier. Every test gives a tier, so the first test also places new players.

Tiers are off by default. Updating ShyamDuels changes nothing for your players until you enable them in tiers.yml.

Enabling tiers on an existing server

  1. Update the plugin and start the server once. It creates plugins/ShyamDuels/tiers.yml and adds the new messages to lang/messages.yml (your edits are kept).
  2. Open tiers.yml and set enabled: true.
  3. Pick what menus, chat, tab, nametags and scoreboards show with display-mode:
    • DIVISIONS: keep the ELO division ranks (default).
    • BOTH: show the tier in front of the division rank. Good for a transition period.
    • TIERS: show tiers only.
    The existing <rank> and <rank_colored> scoreboard/tab tokens and the above-name kit rank follow this setting, so you do not have to edit your templates.
  4. Optional: choose the kits under kits.include / kits.exclude. With an empty include list every kit that has ranked queue enabled gets tiers.
  5. Optional: set rating.seed-from-elo.enabled: true so experienced players start from a rating based on their current kit ELO instead of 1500. Tiers themselves are only earned by tests.
  6. Run /shyamduels reload-tiers (or restart).

Players' ELO, divisions and stats are not touched. You can turn tiers off again at any time; tier data stays stored.

Rating and placement

Every tracked kit has a rating that starts at 1500. After a counted 1v1 match the winner gains and the loser drops by K × (1 − expected score), where the expected score is 1 / (1 + 10^((opponent − self) / 400)). K is 32, or 48 during the first 5 matches in a kit (shown as "Unplaced").

Which matches count is set under counted-matches: ranked queue, normal /duel fights and rematches count by default, unranked queue does not, tier tests do. Party, FFA, tournament, bot and custom-kit matches never count. rating.same-opponent-daily-limit (default 10) stops two players farming rating against each other.

Readiness bars

Shown in /tiers on each kit page. A tier test opens when every required bar is full.

Hover a section to see how to raise it; click it for a page explaining what it shows, how to raise it and why it matters. /tierinfo opens the same menu as /tiers.

BarMeasuresFull atRequired
SkillKit ratingThe rating of the tier above the one it reachesNo: it decides the tier a test gives
ConfidenceCounted matches played15Yes
CompetitionAverage strength of opponents faced60%No

ChallengeWin rate against equal or higher rated opponents50%No

readiness.confidence.count decides what fills the confidence bar: MATCHES (default) or WINS, for an unlock rule like "3/5 wins to unlock tier test". The texts switch to their -wins versions and the action bar to tiers.progress-bar-unlock-wins.

Bar colours go red, yellow, light green, green as they fill. Targets, starting values and which bars are required are set under readiness. For the older ladder where a test only opens once the rating reaches the next tier, set skill.required and challenge.required to true and tier-test.skill-placement to false.

Tier tests

  • An eligible player who queues a ranked 1v1 kit gets a Tier Test Ready page that says what a win and a loss give: Accept Challenge or Reject Challenge (back without queueing). Set tier-test.prompt-normal-queue: true to show Queue Normally instead, which joins the normal queue and skips the prompt for 15 minutes. They can also use the ⚔ Tier Test button in the queue menu, /tiers test <kit> or the button on the kit page.
  • Each counted match ends with one chat line: the rating change and the next step, for example Sword: 1970 rating (-30). Next tier test after 2 more ranked matches.
  • Tests are first-to-tier-test.first-to (default 3), independent of your normal round settings. When a test starts, the challenger gets Tier test for HT3 started: first to 3. and a staff tester is told who they are testing (tiers.test-started, tiers.test-started-tester).
  • tier-test.opponents decides who a challenger meets: CHALLENGERS (another eligible player testing for the same tier), TESTERS (staff only) or BOTH (whoever is available first, default). Testers never meet each other.
  • Staff with shyamduels.tiers.tester wait as testers with /tiers tester <kit> (several kits at once are allowed). By default a tester's own rating does not change in test matches (tester-rating-changes).
  • With tier-test.skill-placement: true (default) a win gives one tier above the rating's tier and a loss the rating's tier (at least the lowest tier). With false a win is the next tier and a loss keeps the tier.
  • After any test the player plays matches-between-tests (default 3) ranked matches before the next one. retry-cooldown-minutes (default 0) can add a wait after a lost test; when both apply, menus and chat show them in one line.
  • In a test, and in any match that needs two or more round wins, a player one round win from the match sees a Match Point! title instead of FIGHT at the start of the round. When both sides are at match point, both see it. Texts: duel.title.match-point in messages.yml; switch and timing (ticks): duels.round.match-point in config.yml.
  • Eligibility is checked again right before the match starts, so admin changes in between cancel the test cleanly.

Ranked match chat

While tiers are on, match-chat in tiers.yml decides what ranked matches in tier kits print. Other kits and servers with tiers off keep the division Elo lines.

  • division-elo-on-start (false): the opponent's division Elo and rank on the Ranked Match card.
  • tier-on-start (true): the opponent's tier and tier rating on that card (tiers.prematch-tier in messages.yml).
  • division-elo-in-summary (false): the ELO: 1359 [-32] line in the match summary.
  • tier-result (true): the tier rating line after each counted match, with the next step towards a test.
  • progress-bar (true) and progress-bar-seconds (4): an action bar after each counted match with a bar that moves from the old to the new value: gained progress shows green, lost progress red while it shrinks. It appears in the arena the moment the match is won and glides to its new value. With progress-bar-shows: AUTO (default) it shows the next step while no tier test is open (3/5 PLACEMENT MATCHES, 8/15 MATCHES TO UNLOCK TIER TEST, 1/3 MATCHES TO NEXT TIER TEST) and otherwise the tier your rating reaches with how far you are toward the next one (LT4 92% MT4). RATING always shows the rating bar; READINESS shows progress toward a tier test. progress-bar-move-ticks (30) sets how long it glides and progress-bar-colors the gained, lost and empty colours. The bar is drawn with tinted block sprites (progress-bar-sprite, progress-bar-segments, default 40), so it looks solid on Java 1.21.9+; Bedrock players get a text bar. Text: tiers.progress-bar in messages.yml.

Tier drops (optional)

Off by default, like the reference servers: once earned, a tier stays.

  • demotion.inactivity: drop tiers tiers for every days without a counted match in that kit. Never below the lowest tier.
  • demotion.losses: after a counted loss, drop one tier when the rating is more than rating-margin below the current tier's rating.

Ranked queue pairing

While tiers are on, ranked 1v1 queues of tracked kits pair players by tier rating. The allowed gap starts at matchmaking.start-range (150) and grows by range-step (25) every step-seconds (5) of waiting, so nobody waits forever. Set matchmaking.enabled: false to keep plain first-come pairing.

Leaderboards

A background job rebuilds the tier boards every leaderboard.refresh-seconds (60) and skips the rebuild when nothing changed. Menus and placeholders only read the result, so they never wait for the database.

  • Kit boards: sorted by tier, then rating. size-per-kit entries (500).
  • Overall board: each kit's tier gives points; a player's score adds their best best-kits-per-category (3) kits in each kit category. The score maps to an overall tier: LT5 at 1 point up to HT1 at 500. The top size-overall (10,000) players are kept.
  • /tiers top opens the board with kit tabs, pages, a search box and filters for country and client (Java / Bedrock).

Menus

Tier menus use Paper dialogs (1.21.7+). Bedrock players get a chat summary for /tiers. Text and layout are in dialog-menus/tiers.yml.

  • Statistics: /stats shows a card with Elo (and progress to the next rank), win rate and K/D bars, streaks, and links to per-kit stats and tiers. Per-kit stats list every kit with Elo, record and K/D; hover a row for the full numbers. Text is in dialog-menus/stats.yml and kit-stats.yml.
  • /tiers [player]: overall tier, points and rank, and one button per kit. A kit page shows tier, peak, rating, rank, record, test history and the five bars.
  • Queue menu (dialog mode): tabs for All, Favourites and your kit categories, live queued/in-game counts, a tier test progress bar per kit, Queue All / Leave All, Keep Queuing (rejoin your queues after each match) and Edit Favourites. Text is in dialog-menus/queue.yml.
  • Duel menus (dialog mode): /duel without a name opens a player search with up to 9 online suggestions. After picking a kit, an options menu shows both players' tier or rank and a rounds slider. A confirmation appears after sending, with a "Don't show again" button. Text is in dialog-menus/duel-flow.yml.

Favourites, Keep Queuing and "Don't show again" are stored per player in the database table player_preferences.

Storage and performance

  • Tier data is stored in the table tier_profiles (one row per player and kit) in the stats database, on SQLite or MySQL. Nothing is kept in per-player files.
  • Online players are kept in memory and saved in the background every storage.autosave-seconds (60), right after every tier test and admin change, and on quit and shutdown.
  • In our tests a leaderboard rebuild over 100,000 players (200,000 rows) took about 0.8 seconds on a background thread. With 40 players online, tick times were the same with tiers on and off.

Developer events

TierChangeEvent (reasons TEST_PASSED, TEST_PLACED for a lost test that still raised the tier, ADMIN, INACTIVITY, LOSSES, RESET), TierTestStartEvent and TierTestEndEvent in com.shyamstudio.shyamduels.api.event.tier. All fire on the main thread. Read tiers and boards with ShyamDuelsAPI.tiers(); see Services and Events.
